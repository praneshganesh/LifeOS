# Saavi — features

Saavi is a household app that keeps home life in one place: what you own, who it belongs to, what you spend, what repeats, and what’s due.

---

## Home screens

### Today
What’s overdue, due today, and coming up — reminders, habits, and classes. Mark items done or snooze them. Swipe right to complete, swipe left to snooze where supported.

### Life
Primary tiles: Things, Expenses, Subscriptions, Classes, Habits, and Tasks.
Under **More**: Household, Documents, Vehicles, Warranties, Insurance, Purchases, Maintenance, and Reminders.

### Ask
Type to Saavi. Ask questions or give instructions (for example, add a reminder or log an expense). One message and reply uses one Talk turn when AI is involved.

### Talk
Speak to Saavi. Same idea as Ask, by voice. One spoken ask uses one Talk turn when AI is involved.

### Capture
Take a photo, pick from the library, or upload a file.
- On-device text reading can fill fields from a receipt or ID.
- Optional **Identify with AI** can name a product from a photo (uses one Talk turn).
- After the photo: check the name (and brand), add optional details if you want, then save.
- Receipts can be saved as a Thing, an Expense, both, or a Document.

You can also add a Thing by typing (**+**), without a photo.

## What you can track

| Feature | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Things** | Items at home — name, brand, where they live, who they belong to, price, store, warranty. |
| **Spaces & rooms** | Homes and rooms so Things have a place. |
| **Household** | People and pets; assign Things and activities to them. |
| **Expenses** | One-off spend, merchant, category, receipt photo. |
| **Subscriptions** | Recurring services — amount, cycle, renews on, provider. |
| **Habits** | Daily / weekly check-ins. |
| **Classes** | Lessons or packs (for example kids’ classes), sessions, schedule. |
| **Reminders** | “Last done” style activities — once or repeating, with alerts. |
| **Documents** | Passports, IDs, and similar — number, expiry, reminders. |
| **Warranties / insurance / vehicles / purchases / maintenance** | Views and flows built on Things and related dates. |

Lists that you own support swipe to delete (with confirmation). Derived lists (for example warranties of Things) open the item; they don’t delete by swipe.

## Talk turns (AI)

A **Talk turn** is one AI ask: Talk, Ask, or Identify on a photo.

| Plan | Talk turns per day |
| --- | --- |
| Trial | 25 |
| Pro | 100 |
| After trial / subscription ends | 0 until you subscribe |

Adding or editing by hand, and on-device text reading on photos, do **not** use Talk turns.

---

## Trial and Pro

- **Trial:** 7 days when you start, no card required. Full app access.
- **When trial ends:** Saavi asks you to subscribe to continue.
- **Pro:** Apple subscription (monthly or yearly). Unlimited Things and home data; Talk limited as above.
- **Cancel:** iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancel stops the next renewal; it does not refund the period already paid.
- **Restore purchases:** Settings → Plan, if you reinstall or change phones.
- **Sign in with Apple:** Settings → Account — so identity and subscription can restore on a new phone.
- **Delete account:** Settings → Account — removes your login and this phone’s copy. Does not cancel Apple billing.

---

## Settings (launch)

- **You:** Account, Plan, Household  
- **App:** Notifications, Face ID & lock, Appearance, Home & currency  
- **Legal:** Privacy & terms  
- **Advanced (under Account):** Export or wipe phone data  

---

## Offline

Core add / edit / view works without internet. Talk AI, Identify, cloud sync, and photo upload to the cloud need a connection and a signed-in session.
